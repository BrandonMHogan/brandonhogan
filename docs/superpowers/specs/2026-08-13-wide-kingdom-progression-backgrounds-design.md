# Wide Kingdom Progression Backgrounds Design

## Approved full-state master

The approved fully built wide kingdom is `assets/game-source/kingdom/master-wide-full-approved.png` (`3840×1917`). Its runtime candidate is `public/assets/game/kingdom/master-wide-full-approved.webp`.

This image is the immutable visual baseline for all wide desktop progression backgrounds. Do not regenerate, crop, rescale, recolor, rearrange, or paint over approved buildings when producing earlier states. Each earlier state must preserve canvas dimensions and every unaffected pixel as closely as possible.

## Five interactive locations

1. Farm: farmhouse, windmill, fencing, hay, gardens, and multiple separated working plots.
2. Lumber Camp: lodge, timber stacks, saw area, and forest clearing.
3. Town: one interactive settlement represented by a small cluster of buildings led by a warmly lit inn.
4. Quarry: entrance integrated directly into the mountain.
5. Castle: compact castle integrated directly into its plateau.

## Progression background sequence

1. Decrepit Farm at the initial settlement site. The player's first Farm click restores it for free.
2. Farm only.
3. Farm and Lumber Camp.
4. Farm, Lumber Camp, and Town.
5. Farm, Lumber Camp, Town, and Quarry.
6. Fully built kingdom, including Castle—the approved master.

Each state will be produced and visually approved in reverse order, removing one location at a time from the approved master. Runtime integration begins only after all six states are approved.
